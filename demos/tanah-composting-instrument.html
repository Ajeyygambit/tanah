Mathematical Provenance & Governing Formulas

Complete transparent arithmetic substituted with empirical values.

ASSUMPTIONS REGISTER

1. Core Pilot Cash-Flow Formula

potential_kg_day = households × household_size × 1.17 kg × 0.306

Substituted: 200 × 4 × 1.17 × 0.306 = 286.416 kg / day

captured_kg_day = potential_kg_day × participation_rate

Substituted: 286.416 × 0.55 = 157.529 kg / day (13,232.4 kg over 84 days)

net_diverted_kg = captured_kg × (1 − contamination_rate)

Substituted: 13,232.4 × (1 − 0.08) = 12,173.8 kg = 12.174 tonnes

cost_per_tonne = total_cost / net_diverted_tonnes

Substituted: RM 4,866 / 12.174 tonnes = RM 399.71 / tonne

2. Steady-State Scaling Model

sites(n) = ceil(n / 250)

tonnes(n) = n × 0.26449

labour_cost(n) = sites(n) × 730 × labour_rate

tooling_cost(n) = sites(n) × 250 / 5

water_cost(n) = sites(n) × 25

supervision(n) = 5200 + 2400 × max(1, sites(n)/10)

3. What this Demonstration Cannot and Does Not Claim

  • Does NOT claim net developer cost savings under paid labour.
  • Does NOT calculate speculative carbon credits without user-supplied regional emissions factors.
  • Does NOT infer national adoption from a single suburban township testbed.
  • Does NOT simulate sensor connectivity or fake IoT telemetry.
